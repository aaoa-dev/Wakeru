The plastic test

Most takeout and konbini packaging comes down to three questions.

  1. Is it 100% plastic?No metal, paper or rubber parts attached. Pump tops and wrap boxes with a cutter fail this.
  2. Is it empty and clean?Give it a quick rinse or wipe. Curry stains, oil or stuck rice that won't come off fail this.
  3. Is it under 30 cm?Bigger than that is oversized garbage (粗大ごみ).
